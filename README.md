# Best Fit Showdown — Unit 3 Stats Test Review

A review game for one student or a group of 2–3 sharing one device, built for the Stats B Unit 3 test
(scatterplots, correlation, causation vs. lurking variables, explanatory/response variables,
segmented bar graphs, slope and intercept, residuals and predictions). Each student must answer at least 15 questions on their own turns (usually about an hour).

## How to open it
- Open `index.html` in any web browser, or
- Turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main`, folder `/`) and share the link with students.

## How it plays
- Six topic columns, each with 1-, 2- and 3-point tiles (18 in all). Each player can play each tile once; played tiles are marked ✓ or ✗ and locked, and colored dots show who has played each tile. No question repeats during a game.
- Students explain their reasoning out loud before answering; a partner can steal a missed question for half the points.
- Written-answer cards are scored by partners using an on-screen checklist.
- Two Team Rounds (when everyone has answered 5 questions, and again at 10) have everyone work a full test-style problem on paper, then check it against a key.
- The results screen shows which topics the group should review.
- **Solo mode:** no steals, students score their own written answers, and Team Rounds become Challenge Rounds. The goal is to score in all six categories and beat 40 points.

## Completion codes
A code only appears once every player has answered at least 15 questions on their own turns, right or wrong.
Steals and Team Rounds earn points but don't count toward the 15.

The results screen shows a code like `BF-3K9Q-0T2M-X7BD`. Students write it down with their names.
To check one, open the game, expand **"Teacher: check a completion code"** on the start screen, and enter the code and names.
It shows, for each student, how many questions they answered, how many they got correct, their percent, and whether they met
the 15-question minimum, plus the date, active minutes played, and Team Rounds finished.
